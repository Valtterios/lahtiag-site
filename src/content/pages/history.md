## Past events

### LahtiAG × Sakura: Game Fest

Game Fest gathered gamers at the Event Arena, Mukkula Campus, for a full day
of fun, friendly competition, and community. From exciting tournament matches
to casual gaming sessions, there was something for everyone to enjoy.

The atmosphere was full of energy, great games, and great company. We had an
amazing time together and can't wait to host the next Game Fest!

A huge thank you to Sakura for hosting Game Fest with us — their support
helped make the event such a success. Go show them some love on Instagram:
[@sakura_lahties](https://www.instagram.com/sakura_lahties)

### Kapital × LahtiAG: Mario Kart Tournament

Our Mario Kart Tournament was a total blast! From the twists and turns of
Mario Kart 8 Deluxe to the nostalgia of Mario Kart Wii, the competition was
fierce, the races were thrilling, and the energy was off the charts.

Congratulations to our champions who sped their way to victory, and to
everyone who participated — you all brought the heat to the track!

Thank you to Kapital, who hosted the very first Mario Kart Tournament in
2025 and helped plan and host this year's tournament. Support Kapital on
Instagram at [@kapital_ry](https://www.instagram.com/kapital_ry) or visit
[kapitalry.fi](https://kapitalry.fi). Looking forward to collaborating again
next year with an even bigger event!
