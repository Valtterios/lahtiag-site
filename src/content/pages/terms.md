These terms apply to tickets sold on lahtiag.fi by Lahti Association of
Gaming LAG ry (business ID 3485167-1, Mukkulankatu 19, 15210 Lahti,
board@lahtiag.fi).

## The ticket

A ticket is personal and admits one person to the event named on it. It is
shown as a QR code on your ticket page; the code is checked once at the
door. Tickets bought while signed in with Discord are kept under **My
tickets**; tickets bought by name, without Discord, are reached only
through the link shown after payment, so keep that page open, bookmark it
or take a screenshot.

Member prices and members-only tickets need a membership linked to the
Discord account you are signed in with.

## Payment

Payments are handled by Stripe Payments Europe, Ltd. on its own pages; the
association never sees your card details. Prices are in euros and include
any applicable taxes. Stripe emails a receipt.

## Cancellation and refunds

Event tickets are for a specific date and are not refundable if you change
your mind. If the association cancels the event, every ticket is refunded
in full to the payment method used. If the event is moved and the new date
does not suit you, ask for a refund at board@lahtiag.fi within seven days of
the announcement.

Under the Finnish Consumer Protection Act, the 14-day right of withdrawal
does not apply to leisure events booked for a specific date.

## Problems

Write to board@lahtiag.fi. Disputes that cannot be settled between us may
be taken to the Consumer Disputes Board (kuluttajariita.fi); the EU online
dispute resolution platform is at ec.europa.eu/odr.
